Privacy Policy for AkScan

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

AkScan ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the AkScan mobile application collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Permissions & Data We Process

  • Camera Permission: Used solely to capture photos of physical documents, receipts, and ID cards when you initiate a scan.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used only to import existing images for scanning and to save generated PDFs or images locally on your device.
  • AI & Document Processing: Document edge detection, OCR, and PDF generation run locally on your device. If you use optional AI assistant features, only the specific text or image you submit is processed to generate your response.

2. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or scanned files to any third parties.

3. Data Retention & Security

Your scanned documents are stored locally on your device and remain under your control until you delete or share them.

4. Children's Privacy

AkScan does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
